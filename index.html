Calorie Counter

How to Use

  1. Enter your daily calorie budget in the Budget field.
  2. Select Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner, or Exercise from the dropdown.
  3. Meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner) add calories, while Exercise burns calories.
  4. Click Add Entry and fill in the Name and Calories for each item.
  5. Use the X button on any entry to remove it if added by mistake.
  6. Click Calculate Remaining Calories to view your result.
  7. Click Clear to reset all entries and start over.
